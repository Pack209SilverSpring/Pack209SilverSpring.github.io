    content: >-
      This page type is meant for simple content pages -- a hero photo, an
      intro, and one or more sections of rich text. It doesn't support custom
      layouts, embedded forms, or anything else that needs real HTML.


      For that kind of page, ask your developer to build a custom template instead.
